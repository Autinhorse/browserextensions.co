# Blog Content Plan

SEO content plan for the browserextensions.co blog, organised as a **pillar +
spoke** cluster around AI Chat Snapper's strengths (10 AI assistants × export
formats × destinations).

## Principles

- **Quality over volume.** A handful of genuinely useful posts beats hundreds of
  thin ones. Mass low-value content risks Google's *scaled content abuse* policy
  and can drag down the whole domain's quality rating.
- **AI to draft, humans to finish.** Use AI for outlines and first drafts, then
  add: 1-3 **real screenshots**, verified steps (AI often gets product steps
  wrong), a genuine recommendation, and a fact check of every product claim.
- **Every post needs one thing a generic AI article can't have** — a screenshot,
  a comparison you actually made, or a specific tested tip.
- **Cadence:** 2-4 posts/week, then adjust based on Search Console data. Do not
  publish dozens at once.

## Platform tiers (by English search demand)

- **Tier 1:** ChatGPT (build the deepest cluster here)
- **Tier 2:** Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek
- **Tier 3:** Grok, Mistral
- **Chinese-market (low English volume):** Qwen, Kimi, Doubao — defer to the
  Chinese locale; write these for zh / Baidu SEO, not English.

## Internal linking rules

- Each spoke links to/from its pillar (two-way).
- Every post links naturally to `/products/ai-chat-snapper`.
- The cluster — not any single article — is what builds topical authority.
