Some conversations are too sensitive to route through another service - client work, private research, anything confidential. The safest way to keep them is offline: a local file generated on your own device, with nothing uploaded.

## Why offline matters

- **Privacy.** Share links and cloud exports send your conversation somewhere else. A local export never leaves your machine.
- **Durability.** A file on your disk survives account suspensions, service outages, and plan changes.
- **No lock-in.** Plain formats like Markdown and TXT stay readable for years, with tools you already own.

## Save an AI chat offline

[AI Chat Snapper](/products/ai-chat-snapper) generates local exports entirely in your browser and can run fully offline:

1. Open the conversation in any [supported assistant](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/supported-platforms).
2. Click the AI Chat Snapper icon.
3. Choose a format - [Markdown](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/export-to-markdown) and [PDF](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/export-to-pdf) are good offline choices.
4. Choose **save locally**.

There's no account, no sign-in, and no upload. See [Local Storage](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/local-storage) for the details.

## Building an offline archive

For an evergreen, private knowledge base, export important chats to Markdown and keep them in a local Obsidian vault. Everything stays on your device, fully searchable, and yours.
