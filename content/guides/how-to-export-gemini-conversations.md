Google Gemini conversations can hold useful research and drafts you'll want outside the chat window. Here's how to export them.

## Method 1: Gemini's built-in options

- **Google Takeout.** Gemini activity can be exported through Google Takeout as part of your Google data. It's a complete archive, useful for backup but slow and coarse for a single conversation.
- **Copy and paste.** Selecting and pasting into Google Docs works, but formatting and code styling often don't survive.

## Method 2: Print to PDF

`Ctrl/Cmd + P` → **Save as PDF**. Quick, but it captures the surrounding interface and breaks up long answers.

## Method 3: One-click export with AI Chat Snapper

[AI Chat Snapper](/products/ai-chat-snapper) exports the Gemini conversation you're viewing in one click:

1. Open the conversation at gemini.google.com.
2. Click the AI Chat Snapper icon.
3. Choose **PDF, Word, PNG, Markdown, TXT, or JSON**.
4. Save it locally, or send it to Notion, Google Docs, or Obsidian.

Headings, lists, and code blocks stay formatted. Since Gemini lives in Google's ecosystem, exporting to [Word and sending to Google Docs](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/export-to-word) is a natural fit.
