AI Chat Snapper installs like any other browser extension. It takes less than a minute.

## Install from the Chrome Web Store

1. Open the AI Chat Snapper listing on the Chrome Web Store.
2. Click **Add to Chrome**, then confirm in the dialog.
3. Pin the extension so its icon stays visible in your toolbar - click the puzzle-piece icon, then the pin next to AI Chat Snapper.

## Supported browsers

AI Chat Snapper works in Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers, including:

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Brave
- Arc
- Opera
- And other compatible browsers.

## Permissions

The extension only reads the AI conversation on the page when you click it to export. Local exports are generated in your browser - see [Local Storage](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/local-storage) for how your data is handled.
