Instead of saving a local file, AI Chat Snapper can send a chat directly to the place you actually work: **Notion, Google Docs, or Obsidian**.

## Available destinations

| Destination | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Notion** | Collecting chats in a workspace or database |
| **Google Docs** | Editable documents you'll share or comment on |
| **Obsidian** | Markdown notes in a local vault |

## Send a conversation to a destination

1. Open the conversation and click the extension.
2. Choose a destination instead of a local file.
3. The first time, you'll connect the destination; after that, sends are one click.

## Privacy

Cloud destinations are **optional and only run when you choose them**. If you never pick a destination, nothing leaves your device. When you do send a chat, it goes directly from your browser to the destination you selected.

For the fully offline option, see [Local Storage](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/local-storage).
