## Export a conversation

1. Open a conversation in any [supported AI assistant](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/supported-platforms) - for example, ChatGPT.
2. Click the AI Chat Snapper icon in your toolbar.
3. Choose a format: **PDF, Word, PNG, Markdown, TXT, or JSON**.
4. Save the file locally, or send it to Notion, Google Docs, or Obsidian.

That's it. The export keeps the structure of your chat - who said what, headings, lists, and code blocks all stay intact.

## Choosing a format

The right format depends on what you'll do next:

- **PDF** - finished conversations to share, print, or archive.
- **Word** - when you want to keep editing.
- **Markdown** - for notes apps and knowledge bases.
- **PNG** - a quick image for visual sharing.
- **TXT / JSON** - plain text or structured data.

Each format has its own page under **Export formats** in the sidebar, starting with [Export to PDF](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/export-to-pdf).
