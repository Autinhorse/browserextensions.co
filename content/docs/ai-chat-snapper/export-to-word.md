Export to Word (`.docx`) when you want to keep editing the conversation - reformatting, trimming, or folding it into a larger document before you share it.

## Export a conversation to Word
<figure>
<img src="/AIChatSnapper/Export to Word.png" alt="Export AI Conversation to Word" width="400" />
<figcaption>Export AI Conversation to Word</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Open the conversation in any supported assistant.
2. Click the AI Chat Snapper icon.
3. Choose **Word**.
4. Save the `.docx` file, or send it to Google Docs.

## What the Word file includes

The document keeps headings, lists, and code blocks as editable text, so you can restyle it with your own template or paste sections elsewhere. Unlike copy-paste from the chat window, formatting and structure survive the export.

## Word vs. PDF

Both formats keep your text selectable and editable. Choose **Word** when you expect heavier formatting changes — restyling, restructuring, or merging into a larger document — since Word's editing tools are more convenient for that than a PDF editor. Choose [PDF](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/export-to-pdf) when the conversation is essentially final and you want a fixed, portable document that looks the same everywhere.
