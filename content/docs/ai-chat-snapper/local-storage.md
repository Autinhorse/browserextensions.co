By default, AI Chat Snapper saves exports as **local files on your device**. This is the most private option: nothing is uploaded, no account is required, and the extension can run fully offline.

## Save a local file

1. Open the conversation and click the extension.
2. Choose a format (PDF, Word, PNG, Markdown, TXT, or JSON).
3. Choose **save locally** and pick where the file goes.

The file lands in your Downloads folder (or wherever your browser saves files), exactly like any other download.

## 100% offline and private

Local exports are generated entirely in your browser:

- Nothing is sent to a server.
- No account or sign-in is needed.
- No analytics, ads, or tracking.

This makes local export the right choice for sensitive conversations - your data never leaves your device.

## When to use cloud destinations instead

If you'd rather send a conversation straight into a notes app or document service, see [Cloud Storage](/docs/ai-chat-snapper/cloud-storage). Those run only when you choose them.
