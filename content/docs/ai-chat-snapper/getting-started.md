Every day, people use AI assistants to research topics, solve technical problems, draft content, analyze documents, brainstorm ideas, and make important decisions. These conversations often contain valuable knowledge, but they are scattered across different platforms and can be difficult to organize, revisit, or reuse later.

AI Chat Snapper helps you capture and preserve those conversations as files you truly own. Export chats from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Grok, and other AI platforms into PDF, Word, PNG, Markdown, TXT, or JSON formats for long-term storage and reuse.

Saved conversations can be used to:

- Build a personal knowledge library
- Archive research and project work
- Create study notes and learning materials
- Document coding solutions and technical discussions
- Share AI-generated insights with colleagues or clients
- Keep records of important decisions and workflows
- Back up valuable conversations for future reference

Instead of leaving your knowledge locked inside chat windows, AI Chat Snapper turns AI conversations into portable, searchable, and reusable assets.
